# UI craft: three directions, one truthful product

The subject is a private, local engineering tool. Its distinctive mechanism is a protected checker around Codex, followed by Jesh’s explicit Keep or Discard. These are three alternative prototypes, not a new production identity and not a winner selected by the implementer.

## Composition
One hero per screen: the mechanical Vault in Instrument, a single review sheet in Glass Studio, the room in Night Workshop. Navigation and technical detail recede. The original UI remains the default. The replay gallery is explicitly historical and read-only; live decisions remain in the existing ticket view with exact-item, evidence-bound Touch ID. History cannot authorize the present.

## Typography and space
Use -apple-system and the system sans stack; monospace only for hashes and paths. Use 48/32/24/16/13px roles, fluid at phone width. Numbers use tabular figures. Headings balance; copy stays short and factual. Align a few strong edges. Give the hero air rather than filling gaps with cards. Small controls remain at least 44px tall and focus is visible.

## Materials
Instrument: graphite #171b1c, raised steel #272e30, warm brass #c5a36b, ink #f0ede7. Glass: mist #e8eef1, white translucent panes, ink #23303b, steel blue #46677c. Night: charcoal #1c1b22, walnut #4a3630, lamp amber #e8bb77, ink #f1e8dd. Risk red #b33a36 is semantic, never a decorative accent. Shadows have direction and depth. Glass is justified by overlap, not spread everywhere.

## Motion and sound
Motion follows recorded ticket transitions only. Spring transforms settle; no linear easing, ambient bobbing, fake typing or busy character at idle. A replay advances at an explicitly labelled accelerated historical cadence. Reduced motion snaps directly to state. Canvas uses demand rendering and pauses while hidden. Sound is optional, muted by default: one quiet Web Audio click when a recorded lock changes to passed, only after user opt-in. No sound or motion implies more checks than the log actually contains.

## Truth and interaction
The same real ticket events drive all three. Timers/commands are derived only from recorded timestamps/tool events; unavailable metrics stay unavailable. Lock states are the existing event projection, including gaps. A door stays locked when the server disallows Keep. Future/live actions are never simulated as successes. Real red-risk and needs-you histories are discoverable separately; no sample tickets are invented. Empty means a genuinely empty filter, clearly labelled.

## Review
Review with frontend-design, Impeccable, Taste critique guidance and ibelick baseline-ui. The explicit brief overrides marketing layouts, random direction selection, downloaded fonts and new component-framework preferences. Capture 1440px and 390px, inspect, fix, repeat three times. Keyboard semantics, contrast, reduced motion and idle behavior matter as much as the still image. No Apple assets, SF Symbols, Munder assets or copied scenes.
