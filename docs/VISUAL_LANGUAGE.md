# Visual language

## Product feeling
Engineering mission control: personal, calm, tactile. A compact control rail surrounds a working bench, not a wall of interchangeable dashboard cards. Workspace labels, engraved rules and connected station shapes provide a sense of place. System fonts only.

## Core metaphor
Work moves through engineering stations. Intake records intent; Understand currently records repository context only. Clarify, Define, Build, Verify and Proof remain dormant, explicitly unavailable and noninteractive. No worker imagery or execution is implied.

## Truth rule
Every active state comes from persisted Mission/context data or a real pending HTTP request. Loading and failures remain distinct from missing context. Mission phase remains DRAFT. The current Mission is the last viewed record, not an executing task; its ID is a local browser preference checked against actual records. Counts come from existing APIs. Needs You has no producer yet and stays truthfully empty.

## Color semantics
Ink/slate surfaces and light neutral text form the base. Blue means selected/current. Amber identifies the attention location; it does not invent an attention item. Red identifies actual errors. Green means observed coordinator/database readiness or successfully persisted context, never overall Mission completion. Tokens live in theme.css.

## Motion semantics
Short view transitions and subtle persisted-record arrival only. Pending operations use text and aria-busy, not fake progress stages. No idle pulsing, typing, walking or bouncing. Reduced-motion disables animation, transitions and smooth scrolling.

## Detail hierarchy
Simple outside, rigorous inside. Context totals and revision are primary; changes, inventory, complete hashes, IDs, limits and fingerprints are native keyboard-accessible disclosures. Original user intent stays exact in the detail view; previews are display-only.

## Munder boundary
The local research inspiration matrix favors visible genuine work and original state-derived stations. We take sense of place, spatially legible progression and the prospect of grounded activity/presence later. We reject its office metaphor, layout, branding, characters, LimeZu assets, sprites, game mechanics, fake movement and orchestration. All shapes are original CSS/SVG; no source or art is copied.
