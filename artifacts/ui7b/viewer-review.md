Three fresh read-only AI sessions, each receiving only the current unlabelled A–J sheet at 24/48px, light/dark. They were asked for literal appearance, not given status meanings. These are AI observations, not a human usability study. Each viewer saw the refined result of the preceding pass.

## Changes between passes

1. Shared smooth circular perimeter and magnifier replaced the gear-like amber seal. Viewer 1 recognized the magnifier, but identified ambiguous working and accepted symbols.
2. Replaced double tick with underlined tick; enlarged optical question/dot details. Viewer 2 still saw an inner swirl and a near-duplicate checked symbol.
3. Moved working arc onto the perimeter with a faint stationary remainder; replaced accepted underline with a visible open tray. Viewer 3: “None looks like a gear or bottle cap. No pair is fully indistinguishable at 24px in this image; A and F are the closest pair.”

Pause bars and rejection cross are intentionally conventional symbols; the final viewer's II/X descriptions are recorded honestly. No C-like glyph remains. Every mark shares the same circle geometry and stroke weight; accessible status labels remain authoritative. Real event-bound motion is unchanged.
